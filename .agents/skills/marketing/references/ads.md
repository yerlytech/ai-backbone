# Ads

Paying to be seen. Fast, and it stops the day the money stops. Never a single
cent without the maintainer's yes.

## Before any money

- **A page that converts.** Ads that send people to a page nobody acts on pay
  for a leak. Fix the page and the listing first (`copy.md`, `search.md`).
- **The number being bought.** Cost per install, per sign-up or per sale, and
  what one customer is worth. If the second is not known, the first test is
  small and its budget is the price of learning it.
- **How the result is counted, decided now.** The ad platform's own count, the
  store's count, a coupon, a link parameter. Adding a tracking pixel or a
  third-party script to the product is a spec of its own, and the privacy
  pages change with it.

## Channels

| Channel | Reaches | Fits |
|---|---|---|
| Search ads | people typing what you solve | a clear need people search for |
| App store search ads | people searching the store | apps; the cheapest intent there is |
| Feeds and video platforms | people by interest, place and age | something visual, impulse, local |
| Communities and newsletters | a group that trusts its host | a niche audience |

Start with one channel, the one where the people in the context file already
are.

## A test

- One audience, two or three creatives, a fixed daily budget, a fixed end date.
- Change one thing at a time; otherwise nobody can say what worked.
- Let it run long enough to tell: a week at least, more for small budgets.
  Stop the losers, keep the winners, and write down why.
- A creative: a hook in the first line or second, one message, one call to
  action, the context's voice. Made for each platform's format, not one
  resized.
- Read the platform's policies before writing: regulated categories, claims
  it refuses, what it asks to verify.

## The plan file

`brain/06-marketing/YYYY-MM-DD-ads-topic.md`, approved by the maintainer
before it starts:

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| Goal | sign-ups from one city |
| Channel and audience | one each |
| Budget | per day and in total, with the currency |
| Dates | start and end |
| Creatives | the two or three, in the file |
| What is counted, and where | the store's installs, by campaign link |
| Stop rule | cost per sign-up above a stated amount after a stated spend |

After it ends: what it cost, what it brought, what was learned, in the same
file. The numbers go to the data analyst.
